### 2. Prototype Submission Details
- **Project Name:** `PoolPilot`
- **One-Line Pitch:** `A high-performance, non-custodial AMM token swap and liquidity protocol featuring real-time constant-product math (x·y=k), WebSocket tickers, and a live transparent activity ledger.`
- **Project Description:**
  > PoolPilot solves the DeFi "black box" problem where traders suffer unexpected slippage and opaque fee extraction. Built on a non-custodial constant-product automated market maker ($x \cdot y = k$), PoolPilot computes exact quotes and 30-basis-point liquidity provider fees down to the wei. 
  > 
  > Key architectural highlights:
  > - **Atomic On-Chain Settlement:** Sub-second constant-product swaps with customizable slippage tolerances (0.1%, 0.5%, 1.0%).
  > - **Real-Time Pool Activity Ledger:** Live streaming of all swaps, mints, and burns with 1-click clipboard verification, block finality badges, and CSV export.
  > - **Real-Time Price Discovery:** Multi-pair WebSocket live ticker streaming directly from market feeds.
  > - **Institutional-Grade Security:** Hardened against first-depositor share inflation attacks (permanent dead-address locking), reentrancy guards, and strict Checks-Effects-Interactions pattern—validated across 35 automated invariant unit tests.
- **Tech Stack:**
  `Solidity (v0.8.20), Hardhat, Foundry, React (v18), Vite, TailwindCSS, Ethers.js, Lucide Icons, Binance WebSocket API`
- **Prototype / Demo Link:** `https://poolpilot.vercel.app` *(or your deployed Vercel/Netlify URL, or localhost demo)*
- **GitHub Repository:** `https://github.com/Piby3/PoolPilot`
